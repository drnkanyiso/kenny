## Ancestral knowledge

Dr. Nkanyiso describes himself as a traditionalist, herbalist, and spiritual practitioner. His stated approach draws on African traditional values, religious beliefs, and clan customs. Ancestors and spirits are an important part of his worldview.

## Spiritual practice

He describes using divination, rituals, prayer, chants, and spellwork as forms of spiritual practice. He says people approach him about love and family concerns, business, lost items, addiction, fertility, debt, financial instability, men's sexual wellbeing, political aspirations, gambling, and other personal matters. These are descriptions of spiritual services and beliefs, not guaranteed outcomes or professional advice.

## His stated mission

His larger mission, as he describes it, is to build a bridge between ancestral wisdom and modern science. He says his college education and indigenous knowledge give him perspective across both worlds.

## Health and wellbeing

His supplied biography also refers to herbal remedies and health conditions. This website does not claim that herbal or spiritual practices diagnose, treat, or cure illness. For arthritis, respiratory or heart conditions, diabetes, skin conditions, anxiety, depression, addiction, fertility concerns, or any other health issue, consult an appropriately qualified health professional.
