Losing hard-earned belongings or experiencing theft can be distressing. Dr. Nkanyiso offers spiritual consultation about missing household items, electronics, business goods, shipments, educational documents, and animals.

Share what was lost and any known circumstances or possible location. Recovery is not guaranteed, including within a few hours or 24 hours; spiritual consultation does not replace reporting theft or using practical recovery channels.
